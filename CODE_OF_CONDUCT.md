# Code of Conduct

Umbra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and give feedback on the work, not
the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/AmineChr54/umbra/security/advisories/new), or by contacting
[@AmineChr54](https://github.com/AmineChr54) directly. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer
may remove content and block people who break these rules.
