General

How Umbra starts and behaves on your desktop.

Start with Windows
Your widgets appear on the desktop when you sign in.
Refresh interval
How often widgets fetch fresh data.
Animations
Chart draw-in, counters and pulses. Also follows Windows' animation setting.
Check for updates
Once a day Umbra asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. Nothing is installed without your say-so.
Hide during fullscreen apps
Widgets disappear while a fullscreen game or video is in the foreground and come back afterwards.
Peek duration
The Peek hotkey (set it under Hotkeys) raises widgets above other windows for this many seconds, without taking focus.
Drag a widget anywhere on the desktop to move it. To resize it, turn on Edit mode in My widgets or the tray menu. Double-click it to open the source. Umbra lives in the tray.

My widgets

Turn widgets on or off and change their settings. Changes apply instantly.

Edit mode
Arrange widgets on the desktop: drag to move, drag the corner handle or Ctrl+scroll to resize. Locked widgets stay as they are. Edit mode turns off when Umbra restarts.
Community

Widgets made by people like you

Every widget runs in a sandbox and can only reach the sites it declares. Built one? Open a pull request and it shows up here for everyone.

Themes

Colors and corner radius for every widget. Themes are plain data with no code, so they can't run anything or load files from the internet.

Made a theme? Add themes/<id>/theme.json in a pull request and it shows up here for everyone.

Hotkeys

System-wide shortcuts that work while any app has focus. Click a shortcut, then press the keys (at least one of Ctrl, Alt or Win). Esc cancels, Backspace turns it off.
